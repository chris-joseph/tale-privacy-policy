Tale – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Tale ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. We are committed to keeping your travel planning data safe, private, and secure.

1. Information We Collect

To help you plan your travels and keep your itineraries up to date across all your devices, we collect the following information:

A. Account Information

  • Log In Details: When you sign up or log in, we collect your email address, chosen username, and password. You can also sign in easily using your Google account.
  • Friend Connections (Future feature): We are working on a "Travel Buddy" feature. When this becomes available, you will be able to connect with friends in the app to plan trips together.

B. Location Information

  • Your GPS Location: With your permission, the app accesses your device's location to automatically tag the coordinates of your travel stops on the map.

C. Photos & Camera Access

  • Pictures and Avatars: The app accesses your device's gallery or camera so you can select and upload profile avatars, trip cover images, or add photos to your travels.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Keep your travel itineraries and packing lists updated and saved across your devices.
  • Securely log you in and manage your account.
  • Help you tag and display locations on your travel map.
  • Monitor app performance, fix errors, and improve your overall experience.

3. Data Storage & Safety

  • Saving Your Data: Your plans (trips, stops, activities, and packing lists) are saved automatically on secure online servers so they are never lost.
  • Photo Storage: Uploaded photos (like cover pictures and profile avatars) are stored in secure online storage.
  • Keeping It Secure: We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or theft. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Third-Party Services

We partner with a few trusted service providers to run the app:

  • Sign-In Services: We use Google Sign-In to allow secure logins.
  • Map & Location Services: Map providers help show your location and tag your travel stops.
  • Performance Tracking: We use tools from Firebase to track performance and catch crashes so we can keep the app running smoothly.

5. Your Choices & Controls

You are in control of your data:

  • Permissions: You can grant or turn off location, camera, or gallery permissions at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Account Deletion: You can choose to delete your account and all of your saved travel data from our systems at any time.
  • Editing Information: You can edit your profile details and trip itineraries directly inside the app.

6. Policy Changes

We may update this policy as we add new features. We will post any updates here and change the "Last Updated" date.
